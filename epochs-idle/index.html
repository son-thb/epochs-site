Epochs: Idle Civilization

From a fire and a few huts to marble cities

A calm idle strategy game about guiding one small settlement through four ages of history. Your town keeps working while you're away.

Get it on Google Play

Android · English, Tiếng Việt, 한국어, Español

What you do

  • Build your town

    Place huts, farms, workshops and storehouses on a growing map. Keep your people fed, housed and working.

  • Research the ages

    Discover 34 technologies. Every invention unlocks new buildings, more land and new goods.

  • Raise wonders

    Build Stonehenge, the Hanging Gardens, the Library of Alexandria and the Colosseum, then upgrade them for lasting bonuses.

  • Explore the world

    Send scouts to river bends, salt marshes and olive coasts to find clay, tin, salt, wine and other goods.

  • Daily expeditions

    Lead a party into the fog three times a day: find supplies, dodge hazards and face armed strangers.

  • Plays while you're away

    Come back to full stores, finished buildings and scouts with news. It also plays fully offline.

Four ages

About two weeks of relaxed play to reach the end of the Classical Age.
